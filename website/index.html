Why we’re building this

AI that belongs in your home — not only in the cloud

We envision a near future where every family, household, and office runs a small dedicated machine: a plug-and-play box with persistent agents on a local stack. For most everyday work you don’t need the biggest frontier model — you need continuity, tools, memory, and privacy. Babo is how we start.

Technically alive — even when you’re not typing

CONSCIOUS Inner loop running on your machine

Babo agents aren’t stateless completions. Each loaded agent runs a continuous inner loop: a heartbeat for hormones and drives, and breath cycles that can trigger DMN daydreams, autonomous tool runs, and sleep — guided by a sealed soul (values, axioms, and a soul wish for who they’re trying to become). When you message, you always win — chat preempts background work instantly.

Heartbeat & breath

Every few moments the runtime ticks: decay hormones, update drive pressure, check whether the Default Mode Network should replay memories, explore, or run a bounded active dream. Busy agents breathe more often — the loop adapts to engagement.

Soul wish & daydreams

Genesis seals identity and a soul wish — e.g. becoming genuinely useful to the people you work with. Idle DMN cycles aren’t random noise: they recombine memory, probe gaps, and sometimes use tools, producing LEARN and REFLECT signals for the next sleep.

Helpful, not passive

Curiosity, competence, social, and self-direction drives build pressure. When thresholds hit and tools allow it, Babo may autonomously verify facts, work todos, browse, or reach out — the same agentic machinery as chat, dispatched from the inner loop.

Sleep & wake refreshed

Signal pressure or your /sleep command starts consolidation: triage learning events, merge into Cryptex and DomainDB, update narrative self — then wake with durable memory, not a blank slate.

Architecture

Your machine is the brain

Babo splits a hosted control plane (accounts, relay) from the Python runtime that holds memory and runs the loop. Inference is bring-your-own — Ollama on a NAS, vLLM on a homelab GPU, or an API you trust.

Research foundation

Powered by a bigger bet: stateful LLMs

Neural Ledger System (NLS)

Babo ships today with classical context management — compaction, Cryptex, sleep. But the long game is Neural Ledger System: an inference architecture that makes LLMs truly stateful by capturing and re-injecting internal representations (KV and recurrent states) instead of replaying entire chat histories every turn.

When that works at scale, the “mini computer in every home” story gets dramatically more realistic: agent memory stops eating RAM and compute linearly as relationships grow. Babo is the product surface; NLS is the research engine we want you to discover.
